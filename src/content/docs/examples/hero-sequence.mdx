The title, the lead and the buttons of a hero enter one after the other. Each part has its own `Reveal`, and each `delay` is larger than the previous one.

<Example useCase="sequence" />

## Why a `Reveal` for each part

One `Reveal` with `stagger` on the whole hero would animate the buttons as one element. With a `Reveal` for each part, the buttons get their own `stagger`, and each part can have its own keyframes.

## Tips

- The delays work because the parts enter the viewport together. Above the fold, they enter when the page loads. A `Reveal` that enters later starts its delay at that time.
- Keep the sequence short. The last part starts two gaps after the first one, so a long gap makes visitors wait for the buttons.
- The title is often the Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) element. Chrome skips elements at opacity 0 when it measures LCP, so a hidden title can delay LCP. If the title is your LCP element, keep it out of the sequence.
